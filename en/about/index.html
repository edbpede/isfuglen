About

About the tool and who is behind it.

What it is

A tool for union representatives and club committees in Ishøj Lærerkreds. It does one thing: it takes the notes you have already written and lays them out as a newsletter that looks like the branch has a designer on staff.

Who it is for

A teacher with a busy day, a pile of notes from a club meeting and ten minutes to spare. Not a designer. That is why there is no font picker, no colour picker and no line-height setting — deliberately. You cannot make an ugly newsletter with this.

What it deliberately does not do

  • No login, no account, no cloud.
  • No simultaneous editing and no sharing through the tool.
  • No artificial intelligence. The recognition is rules you can read and correct.
  • No tables, images or free colour choice. This is not a word processor.

How it is built

Astro and Svelte, built to static files. There is no server behind it, and that is not a cost saving — it is the only way to be able to promise that your text goes nowhere.

The logo

The mark is the branch's own: the Danmarks Lærerforening roundel and the "Ishøj Lærerkreds" wordmark. The file sits in the tool unmodified. It is never recoloured, cropped or stretched — the width is set, and the height follows.
